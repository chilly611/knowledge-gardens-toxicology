Incumbents

Verify the law, not the science

Harvey ($15.5B), Legora ($5.6B), Clio/vLex ($5B), Westlaw, Lexis. KeyCite, Shepard's Verify and Legora's new citator all check legal citations against legal corpora. None retrieves or version-pins IARC, ATSDR, EPA IRIS or cohort literature.

Procedural and defense-tilted

Daubert Tracker, Expert Witness Profiler, Lexis Context, Westlaw Profiler, Expert Radar, Pre/Dicta answer "what happened to this expert before." None assesses whether the current opinion's sources are sound.

Why provenance is now the product

Courts have started excluding experts for what their citations cannot show.

2,046

court decisions identifying AI hallucinations as of Sept 21, 2026, up from 719 in January. Fifteen involve expert witnesses.

143

federal standing orders on AI use plus nine circuit instruments (FJC, May 2026); 34 states with guidance, 16 with statewide rules.

1 in 277

new scientific papers contains a fabricated citation (Lancet, May 2026), up from 1 in 2,828 in 2023. Peer review is no longer a safe tier on its own.

17% · 33%

hallucination rates measured for Lexis+ AI and Westlaw AI-Assisted Research (Stanford RegLab, tested May 2024, peer-reviewed 2025).

Excluded · Kohls v. Ellison (D. Minn., Jan 2025) · Concord v. Anthropic (N.D. Cal., May 2025)

Expert declarations struck for nonexistent or misattributed articles. "Credibility destroyed." Manual cite-check missed it.

Admitted · Ferlito v. Harbor Freight (E.D.N.Y., Apr 2025)

The expert could show exactly what each source said and that a human verified it. That showing is the packet.

Discoverable · CLF v. Shell (D. Conn., May 18, 2026) · proposed FRE 707

An expert's AI prompts ordered produced as methodology. FRE 707 would put machine-generated evidence under Rule 702. Build the audit log first.

Sources: Charlotin AI Hallucination Cases database (Sept 21, 2026); The Legal Stack judicial response report 2026; STAT May 7, 2026; Magesh et al. 2025; Mayer Brown and Dechert alerts, June 2026.

The chambers-ready bar · what 2023 to 2026 exclusions demanded

Judges have written the spec for us. Ten things a proponent must show.

01   Realistic dose reconstruction tied to the plaintiff's actual pathway, duration and route

02   Exposure-to-endpoint fidelity: studies match the exposure window and the diagnosed outcome

03   Systematic, not selective, literature review; contrary studies and authors' caveats addressed

04   Confounders named study by study

05   Bradford Hill applied transparently, temporality treated as mandatory

06   Differential etiology that rules out alternatives with plaintiff-specific facts

07   Independent methodology, not a framework handed over by counsel

08   Everything disclosed in the report; no new rationales at deposition

09   Provenance of tools and inputs, including any AI prompts

10   Regulatory classification kept separate from causation at the plaintiff's dose

Acetaminophen MDL (S.D.N.Y., Dec 2023): five experts out for confounding, cherry-picking

Baby Food MDL (N.D. Cal., Feb 27, 2026): seven out; counsel-built exposure model a "black box"

Engilis v. Monsanto (9th Cir., Aug 2025): expert out for undocumented differential etiology

Sommerville (4th Cir., 2025): the outlier stands; cert denied May 26, 2026. The circuit split persists

Amended FRE 702 (Dec 1, 2023): the proponent shows reliability by a preponderance; five circuits now apply it. Washington (Erickson) is Frye, so the packet renders both. Lane 02.

Global decisions · product and verification

An evidence layer under everyone's workflow.

D1 · Exhibit object plus MCP server

Plugs into Word, CoCounsel, Harvey, Everlaw, Supio, Eve, Filevine, Litify, SmartAdvocate. Not another research chat; we never compete for the seat.

D2 · The verification standard is the moat

Version-pinned source, passage quoted, human-adjudicated tier, contrary evidence recorded, retraction re-checked at build, signed report anyone can re-run.

D3 · Provenance log ships as an export

Every source, version, decision and generation step, plus a certification mapped to the judge's standing order. After CLF v. Shell it gets produced anyway.

Exhibits follow the Reference Manual on Scientific Evidence, 4th ed. and the ten requirements from recent exclusions. Regulatory class labeled apart from causation at dose.

Global decisions · buyers, model, beachhead

Priced like expert work, sold to two buyers, proven on one won case.

D7 · Two buyers, one packet

The firm buys per matter. The testifying toxicologist or epidemiologist buys a credential and prior-testimony pack. Nobody sells to the expert today. Testimony history is licensed in from Daubert Tracker, Exlitem, Steno and Lexitas.

D8 · Per-matter fee, expert seat, licensing out

A packet is approved as expert cost, not a software line. Anchor above the $35 to $625 procedural-report ladder and far below a six-figure consulting engagement. Third stream: license the versioned claim layer to AI, legal and clinical platforms.

D9 · Beachhead: PCB / Erickson plus one federal MDL

Sky Valley has a $185M reinstated verdict and a public appellate record. Add PFAS/AFFF (15,222 pending) or Roundup, where Durnell made causation the only path. Density first, then the next substance.

D10 · Outcome pricing stays an option, for funders

No surveyed legal AI vendor prices on outcomes. Fee-splitting rules block direct contingency share with firms; litigation funders (Legalist, $415M fund) who must price Daubert risk are the clean route.

The counsel workflow, revised from five stages to seven

Two new stages, because that is where experts got excluded.

01

Frame

Theory of harm, substances, pathway, endpoints, Frye or 702

02 · new

Reconstruct

Plaintiff-specific dose model; flags counsel-supplied assumptions

03

Assemble

Claims from the TKG graph, tier badges, version pins, retraction check

04 · new

Contest

Defense attack and counter-evidence side by side, per claim

05

Witness

Credentials, prior testimony, reliance list, own-publication check

06

Certify

Provenance log, standing-order paragraph, verification report

07

File

Chambers-ready PDF with exhibit tabs, plus JSON-LD and MCP export

Why Reconstruct: the Baby Food MDL court (Feb 27, 2026) excluded seven experts because the exposure framework was "created by counsel and handed to the expert wholesale."

Why Contest: the Acetaminophen MDL court (Dec 2023) called result-driven, cherry-picked analysis "not a reliable application of scientific methods." Showing the other side first is the defense.
