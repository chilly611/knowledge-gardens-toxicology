Method. Public pages were captured on September 21, 2026 with a headless browser at 1440×900; cookie banners were dismissed; images are the vendors' own marketing renders or app screenshots as published on their sites. A few sites (Daubert Tracker, Clio Duo, Callidus/StrongSuit, OpenEvidence, Perma.cc) served bot challenges and have no picture; their cards carry links and videos instead.

Videos are third-party walkthroughs (LawSites, Artificial Lawyer TV, Legaltech Hub) or vendor demos found in search results; nothing is embedded here, so each opens in a new tab. Screenshots belong to their respective owners and are shown for internal competitive reference only.
