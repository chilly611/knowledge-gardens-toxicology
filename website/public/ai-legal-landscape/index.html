What we are building

A chambers-ready exhibit packet for an expert's scientific opinions: every claim anchored to a version-pinned source, quoted at the passage, tiered for strength by a human, with the contrary evidence recorded and a verification report the other side can re-run.

No product in this review does that for science. The incumbents verify legal citations; the expert-witness tools record what happened to an expert before; the plaintiff platforms stop at the demand letter.
