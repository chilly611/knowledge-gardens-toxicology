## The walkthrough (≈ 5 min — just follow the spine)

**Hero.** *"The Sky Valley case — PCBs leaked inside a school for years; the teachers suffered brain injury."* The **animated caduceus** (the brand's "living mark") sits beside it. Click **Walk the case**.

**① The story** — *"A building made its teachers sick."* PCBs leaked from light ballasts and caulking at the Sky Valley Education Center; teachers developed neurological/cognitive injury; first three to trial in 2021. *Art: the anatomical specimen plate.* Click **Next**.

**② The science** — *"How a PCB reaches the brain."* PCBs are neurotoxicants — they disrupt thyroid signaling, dopamine systems, and neuronal calcium signaling, producing memory/learning/executive-function deficits. *Art: the animated "Identify" plate.* Optional: **Full PCB profile, with sources** → tiered citations (IARC, ATSDR, EPA). Click **Next**.

**③ The evidence** — *"Three sources behind every claim."* Tiered, Daubert-ready, and the AI reads only from it. Click **Run the live analysis** → the counsel workspace streams a cited read (theory of harm → neurotoxic mechanism → brain injury; Daubert posture; strongest evidence + biggest vulnerability). *Art: the animated "Reflect" plate (the rooted book).* Click **Next**.

**④ The case file** — *"The full record, under lock."* 1,959 documents, gated because they include medical records. Click **Open the case file** (or **Sign in** in the nav) → enter the demo credentials → search and read the corpus. *Art: the animated "Resolve" plate (the wax seal).* Click **Next**.

**⑤ The wider garden** — *"One case. A whole system."* This is the vision beyond Sky Valley:
- **The seven-stage workflow** — identify → assess → plan → act → adapt → resolve → reflect, each a working AI-assisted tool (the seven-plate gallery).
- **Built for four audiences** — the same evidence reframed for a consumer, a clinician, counsel, or a research scientist (the four audience specimens). Lanes: `/flow/consumer`, `/flow/clinician`, `/flow/counsel`, `/flow/researcher`.
- **A family of gardens** — toxicology is the first of many Knowledge Gardens sharing one design and evidence model (the botanical bloom).
